# Portugal operational-progression partner dossier

Portugal remains the preferred second forecast region, but the public ICNF annual burned-area data in the retained corpus is final-only. It has no defensible issue-time revision/publication history.

The executable dossier is generated at `data/validation/data-foundry/external-dependencies/portugal-partner-access-dossier.json`. It requests one representative 2024 operational incident sequence first, with stable incident and revision IDs, native geometry/CRS, observed time, provider publication and modification times, correction/supersession state, source agency, and explicit raw-retention/derived-evaluation/redistribution rights.

Prepared acceptance requires three or more revisions, source-supplied publication times, complete raw hashes and supersession lineage, and at least one 3 h, 6 h, or 12 h label that can be constructed without hindsight. The Raw Vault, bitemporal normalizer, contract, and adapter boundary are ready for a representative payload.

Potential authoritative lanes are an ICNF authenticated operational archive, CEMS Rapid Mapping historical activation products with publication chronology, or separately licensed EFFIS issue-time revisions. Consumer pages will not be scraped, and geometry dates will not be substituted for publication times.

## Prospective access adjudication — 2026-08-25

The official ICNF `BDG/areas_ardidas` ArcGIS service is accessible and queryable, but its stated objective is annual national burned-area cartography. It is not evidence of an operational issue-time perimeter revision stream. The service is retained as final/reference geography only: `https://sigservices.icnf.pt/server/rest/services/BDG/areas_ardidas/MapServer`.

Current occurrence displays do not establish explicit automated-use terms, stable publication timestamps, or a perimeter-revision contract. VIGIA therefore will not scrape them and will not substitute retrieval time, status time, or geometry dates for provider publication time. Portugal prospective capture remains `RIGHTS_AND_PUBLICATION_TIMESTAMP_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; no capture manifest is eligible and no adapter is activated until an authoritative provider or partner supplies the missing contract.

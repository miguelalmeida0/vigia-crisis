# Government pilot scope

In scope: Portugal-mainland incident triage; governed public and partner source acquisition; evidence qualification; map-to-incident continuity; point-based terrain, community, access, and asset context; incident-command objectives and assignments; exercise-only protection review; FieldNet offline continuity; handoff; and audit export.

Out of scope: autonomous public warnings, external brigade dispatch, inferred official confirmation, forecast geometry without scientific admission, exposure intersection derived from a point, causal claims about protection outcomes, and production use of exercise or replay records.

Pilot data must be assigned one universe: `LIVE_PRODUCTION`, `CONTROLLED_EOC_EXERCISE`, or `CERTIFIED_REPLAY`. The evaluation team records every deviation and stops on cross-universe contamination.

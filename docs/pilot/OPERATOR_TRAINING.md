# Operator training

Before evaluation, each operator must demonstrate: selecting a marker and reading Quicklook; switching the same incident across Detail, Intelligence, and Operations; explaining classification, freshness, source-family strength, priority, next system action, and human-decision state; distinguishing thermal support from perimeter; opening a collection plan; assigning and acknowledging operational work; reviewing an exercise protection draft; and completing handoff.

The facilitator must not coach during timed tasks. Record only observed completion, elapsed time, errors, backtracking, trust/clarity/workflow-fit ratings explicitly supplied by the participant, and free-text notes. Blank responses remain unmeasured.

# Pilot acceptance criteria

A pilot-go recommendation requires: one coherent release identity quartet across API, FieldNet, Operator, manifest, and certification; authoritative FieldNet readiness; zero semantic contradictions and dead controls; 100% governed terrain coverage for valid covered coordinates; at least 20 satisfied real requirements across four evidence classes with full transition and latency receipts; safe attention compression below 5%; a completed named-human EOC exercise; fail-closed authority and transport; complete audit export; map/incident continuity; and passing keyboard, responsive, zoom, interaction, truth, and performance gates.

Automatic rehearsal, unit tests, screenshots, provider responses, schemas, and replay records cannot replace the human exercise or partner authority. Any failed condition is a no-go with the exact owner, next step, unlock condition, and evidence reference.

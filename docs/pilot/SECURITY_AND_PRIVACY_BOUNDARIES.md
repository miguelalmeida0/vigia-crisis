# Security and privacy boundaries

Use least privilege, exact incident scopes, authenticated mutations, idempotency keys, and immutable audit receipts. Do not copy partner tokens, operator tokens, FieldNet keys, personal contact data, or raw credentials into screenshots, exports, issue trackers, or certification artifacts.

CAP acquisition is pinned to HTTPS and rejects redirects, unsafe XML declarations, oversized payloads, invalid MIME, schema failures, replay, and unresolved incident bindings. Public-alert and external-dispatch transports remain fail closed unless a named authority and credentialed adapter are explicitly enabled.

Exercise identities must be synthetic role labels. Any real-person usability notes require consent, purpose limitation, access control, and a retention decision by the pilot authority.

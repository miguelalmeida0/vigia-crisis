# Authority and roles

Required roles are Incident Commander, Intelligence Lead, Operations Lead, Protection / Alert Authority, Field / Resource Operator, and Observer / Auditor. One participant may simulate multiple roles only during local exercise and only when each transition is recorded in the operational-period audit history.

The Incident Commander owns objectives and period closure. The Intelligence Lead owns evidence interpretation and escalation. The Operations Lead owns tactics, assignments, resources, deadlines, and acknowledgement. The Protection / Alert Authority alone may approve or reject a protection draft. The Field / Resource Operator records attributable acknowledgements and observations. The Observer / Auditor does not alter truth and exports evidence.

Repository capability, incident scope, operational-period authority, universe, validity window, and transport readiness are all checked. The narrowest constraint wins. Break-glass does not relax evidence or scientific admission.

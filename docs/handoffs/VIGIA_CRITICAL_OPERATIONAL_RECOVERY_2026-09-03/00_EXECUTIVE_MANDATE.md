# 00 — Executive Mandate

## Mission

VIGIA must stop being a polished viewer of stale, weakly verified records and become a credible crisis operating system.

The current runtime foundation is materially stronger: the oversized projection regression was repaired, the compact canonical incident index exists, FieldNet scope is deterministic, exact-incident isolation passed, two cold starts reached READY, and release identity converged. That work is preserved.

The product value is still unacceptable. The current interface exposes 173 records, but the quality layer reports that most are stale and that independent-source, official-confirmation, geometry, weather-context, and forecast-eligibility coverage are effectively absent. Operations is largely an evidence-requirement queue. Outcome measurement has no persisted action-to-outcome chains. System performance is critical while top-level health remains green.

This release must attack every product value below 7.5 with objective evidence. Scores are not raised by copy changes, hiding empty states, renaming statuses, or excluding hard cases without a governed scope rule.

## End-of-day outcome

By the end of this release, VIGIA must demonstrate all of the following:

1. A governed operational incident subset that is current, spatially usable, transparently classified, and not contaminated by stale/history records.
2. Zero cross-route semantic contradictions for incident count, freshness, verification, attention, source state, weather association, map state, and operator work.
3. A real source-resolution engine with identified dependencies, attempts, owners, schedules, deadlines, escalation, and completion criteria.
4. A Command Overview that communicates current verified incidents, new candidates, revalidation burden, and human decisions—not vanity totals.
5. An Incidents and Incident Detail flow that exposes meaningful differentiation, source strength, risk/exposure state, and exact next work.
6. An Intelligence route that creates non-geometric decision advantage even when forecast geometry is scientifically withheld.
7. An Operations route that separates intelligence/source-resolution work from actual response operations.
8. A governed protection workflow, at minimum in explicitly isolated SHADOW/EXERCISE mode where live authority is unavailable.
9. At least one persisted, certified action-to-outcome chain using real historical data or a clearly labelled exercise/replay—not fabricated production outcomes.
10. Map selection, camera, labels, transport state, and performance that meet hard SLOs.
11. Reports that are drillable and operationally useful rather than dashboards of missing capability.
12. A Global Awareness route with correct filter effects, semantic markers, useful Quicklook, and ≥95% geolocation for the operational subset.

## Non-negotiable truth rules

- “Active” means currently operational under an explicit lifecycle rule. It does not mean “record exists” or “not closed.”
- “Operational” describes platform availability only. It may not conceal degraded map transport, stale data, insufficient verification, withheld science, or unresolved operator work.
- “Action” means an initiated or proposed intervention/work item. Evidence requirements are not field operations.
- “Current” is not a universal green state. Platform health, artifact freshness, verification, scientific admission, and work state are separate dimensions.
- A displayed weather product must carry an explicit association scope: incident-associated, regional context, or unassociated.
- A selected incident and its map camera/markers must update atomically.
- “Needs Attention,” “Needs You,” and unresolved high-impact decisions must derive from one canonical workload contract.
- Raw internal enums and canonical plumbing language do not appear in primary operator workflows.
- Missing source coverage is not silently converted to zero confidence or green availability.
- Exercise/replay data is never mixed into production truth.

## Frozen visual scope

Do not redesign the app shell. Preserve:

- white background system;
- navy type and primary hierarchy;
- red incident/action accent;
- current sidebar information architecture;
- current page-header composition;
- restrained borders, radii, and semantic status colors;
- shared combobox/incident switcher visual language;
- current card density where the content remains valid.

Internal route composition may change when necessary to make workflows decisive and comprehensible.

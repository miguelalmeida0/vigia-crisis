# VIGIA operator interview guide

Use this guide for moderated validation with wildfire operations personnel. It is a research instrument, not evidence that a pilot has happened.

## Participants

Recruit 6–10 participants across incident command, dispatch/EOC, field supervision, intelligence/evidence, and prevention. Record role, years of experience, operating context, and prior decision-support exposure. Do not collect incident-sensitive or personnel data in the product.

## Session setup

- Use the certified 4177/4188/4190 release and current canonical data.
- Confirm the participant understands that VIGIA does not replace command authority, dispatch, CAD/AVL, or agency policy.
- Ask the participant to think aloud. Do not teach the screen before the first task.
- Capture task completion, time, first click, wrong turns, questions, confidence, and verbatim reasoning.
- Mark missing integrations as unavailable. Never substitute exercise data without an explicit exercise label.

## Opening questions

1. What information must you establish in the first minute of a new wildfire report?
2. Which source failures or stale observations most often change your decision?
3. How do you currently distinguish “no fire evidence” from “sensor unavailable”?
4. What must survive a shift handoff?
5. Which actions require a named person, authority, or audit trail in your organisation?

## Product probes

- “Tell me what VIGIA knows, what it does not know, and what needs attention.”
- “What would you do next, and which evidence supports that?”
- “What would make this state unsafe to act on?”
- “Which information is observation, inference, operator input, or unavailable integration?”
- “If this image disappeared, what operational context would you still have?”
- “What would you need before trusting this handoff?”

Avoid asking whether the interface is “clear” or “good.” Ask the participant to interpret and act, then compare the answer with the canonical state.

## Closing questions

1. Where did the interface increase or reduce uncertainty?
2. Which label or status could be misread under pressure?
3. What would prevent adoption during a real shift?
4. What belongs in VIGIA, and what must stay in CAD/AVL, dispatch, or agency systems?
5. What is the single highest-value change before a pilot?

## Evidence handling

Store de-identified notes using participant codes. Separate observation from interpretation. Log every severe issue with route, task, state, expected interpretation, actual interpretation, and screenshot or recording timestamp. Do not average away a safety-critical misunderstanding.

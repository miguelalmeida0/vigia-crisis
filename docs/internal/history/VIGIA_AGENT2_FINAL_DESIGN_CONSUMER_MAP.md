# VIGIA Agent 2 final design consumer map

This maps backend intelligence into the ten locked approved screens. It does not authorize a redesign or frontend source change.

| Screen | Primary contract | Secondary contracts | Operator questions and available actions | Blocked/degraded/unavailable behavior | Time, provenance, and UI non-inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Command Overview | `vigia.crisis-decision-packet.v1` | operational snapshot v2, Decision Delta v1, reality snapshot v1, source health | What changed, what is blocked, what needs attention, and which governed work is available? Actions: select incident, acknowledge delivered work, open provenance/replay, request only backend-authorized work. | Consequential actions remain blocked; stale/partial snapshot banner and reasons; no packet means intelligence unavailable, not empty certainty. | Display packet knowledge time, projection token and causing products. Never infer severity, evidence state, priority, authority, source health, rights or action readiness. |
| 2. Incidents | operational snapshot v2 / incident object set | authoritative truth snapshot, CAP lifecycle, source health | Which incidents are current, conflicted, degraded, evidence-poor, or waiting for authority? Actions: filter/select and open governed work. | Rights-filtered incidents remain absent; partial list is labeled; missing official perimeter/label is unavailable. | Preserve list projection/consistency token and as-of time. Never rank incidents or infer official status/perimeter authority in UI. |
| 3. Incident Detail | Crisis Decision Packet v1 | authoritative perimeter sequence, Decision Delta, forecast snapshot, CAP, replay | What is known now, what changed, what is the official perimeter, what could happen, and what decisions are blocked? Actions: drill into evidence/provenance, choose replay cutoff, invoke authorized workflow. | Show official 1h/3h validation unavailable. Sensor progression is a separate layer. Abstained/missing forecast renders no polygon. | Observed, official, sensor-derived and forecast objects retain distinct labels and timestamps. | 6. Actions: dry-run/shadow, execute only when authorized, acknowledge/resolve work, inspect receipts. | Kill switch, cooldown, budget, trust, rights, source health and authority blocks are first-class. Consequential execution remains disabled. | Bind to policy/action/receipt fingerprints and current projection. Never decide readiness, authority, convergence, or postcondition success in UI. |
| 7. Authority & Trust | authority decision v1 | credential/session/device/continuous-trust proofs, transparency/revocation | Who is acting, for what resource/action/scope, with which proof, and why was it allowed/denied? | 8. Actions: approved dry-run/shadow cycle, pause/kill within capability, inspect policy/receipt. | Disabled, authority-required, max-cycle, breaker-open, budget/cooldown and degraded executor states are explicit. | Pin policy fingerprints and cycle time. | 9. | 10. Global Situational Awareness | reality snapshot v1 / authoritative truth snapshot | CAP lifecycle, source health, operational snapshot, forecast snapshot | Where are authoritative incidents and alerts, which sources are healthy, and where is truth conflicted or unavailable? Actions: pan/filter/select and open an incident/provenance record. | Missing geometry is unavailable, not a centroid substitute. Degraded sources and rights gaps are visible. No fake forecast polygon. | Map layers carry source, authority, observed/published/received times and provenance. Never merge incidents, select authoritative perimeter, infer source health, forecast applicability, rights or evidence state. |

## Locked truth rules

- Sensor-derived progression may be shown only as a separately named and styled layer.
- Official 1-hour and 3-hour validation must remain `UNAVAILABLE — TIME_ACCUMULATION_REQUIRED` until legitimate authoritative labels exist.
- An abstained, blocked, or missing forecast produces no forecast polygon.
- A possibility envelope is not an expected perimeter; observed, official, sensor-derived, expected forecast, uncertainty and context layers require distinct text labels and non-color-only styling.
- Every degraded/unavailable state must retain its backend reason. The UI must not replace it with empty success, a placeholder, a fixture, or inferred content.
- The frontend prohibition in `docs/internal/history/VIGIA_AGENT2_CONSUMER_CONTRACTS.md` applies to every screen.
